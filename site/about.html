About Kheti Badi Bazaar

Farming is local.
Possibility shouldn’t be limited.

We’re building a dedicated agricultural marketplace around a simple belief: people in farming deserve a place that understands what they need.

Rooted in the real world
Our purpose

Bring the farm
and the market closer.

A farm’s needs change with every season. Equipment gets upgraded. Harvests need buyers. A new idea needs the right starting point.

Kheti Badi Bazaar brings agricultural categories together in an app designed for finding, listing and saving farm essentials. It gives farmers, individual sellers and dealers a shared place to discover opportunities.

Our focus is practical: useful details, relevant categories and an experience that feels at home in everyday farming.

Who we’re building for

Many roles.
One farming community.

People arrive with different needs. The goal is the same: a more useful connection to the agricultural marketplace.

01 /

Farmers & growers

Discover equipment and inputs for the season, or introduce your farm produce to potential buyers.

02 /

Individual sellers

Give farm equipment, livestock or land a dedicated listing with the details buyers need to explore.

03 /

Dealers & businesses

Present agricultural products in a marketplace organised around the needs of farming.

What guides us

Useful by nature.

Technology should fit the way people farm, search and make decisions.

Relevance over noise

Agricultural categories and meaningful listing details help people find a better starting point.

Room for more voices

English, Hindi, Punjabi and Marathi options in the app help make the experience more familiar.

Clarity at every step

Clear product descriptions and visible listing information support more informed conversations between buyers and sellers.

A growing journey

Built one useful
step at a time.

We’re developing the app with a focus on the core marketplace experience. This website is a place to learn about the product and follow its direction as it grows.
